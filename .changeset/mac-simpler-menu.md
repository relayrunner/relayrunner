A simpler menu. It shows the switch, what this Mac is doing and each running job, then Pause New Jobs, Jobs, Open Dashboard, Settings and Quit, with no icons. The Now, Queue and History tabs, the empty slot rows and the CPU and memory meters are gone: the Jobs window lists running, waiting and finished jobs. There is one pause control, which becomes Resume New Jobs while paused. The offer to take one more job at once moved to the Jobs window. Show Log and Check for Updates moved to Settings › About.
