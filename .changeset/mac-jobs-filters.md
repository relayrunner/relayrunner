The Mac Jobs window opens with the jobs it last showed and refreshes them in the background, and a job you've already opened shows its steps at once. Jobs running or waiting on the Mac come first. Filter by result, repo, workflow and time, and drag the line between the list and the job to resize them. Clicking a job in the menu's History tab opens it in the Jobs window. `relayrunner jobs` lists running jobs first and gains `--repo`, `--workflow`, `--result` and `--since`.
