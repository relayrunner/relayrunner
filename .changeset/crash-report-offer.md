After RelayRunner or its CLI crashes, the next launch offers to send the crash report. It shows everything in it first: the app and macOS versions, the error and where in the code it happened, with no repos, paths or logs. It goes without your name or email unless you tick the box to include them, so we can follow up. Choose Send Report or Don't Send, or tick Don't ask again. Settings › General can turn the offer back on.
