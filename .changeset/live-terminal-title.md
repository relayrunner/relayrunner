`relayrunner connect` now names its terminal tab after the machine and what it's doing, such as "RelayRunner · M3P0 · Waiting for jobs", "Running a job" or "Paused", and puts the old title back when it exits. Press `p` to pause or resume the machine without disconnecting, the same as pausing it on the Runners page.
