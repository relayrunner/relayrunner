---
title: CI traces in your observability tool
date: 2026-11-14
description: Send every workflow run to Honeycomb, Grafana, Axiom or any OpenTelemetry tool as a trace.
---

- **Telemetry.** Send each workflow run to your observability tool as an OpenTelemetry trace, with a span for every job and step and the time each job waited for a runner. Spans also say where each job ran, whether the Router retried it, and the CPU and memory it used on your machines. Set it up in **Settings → Telemetry**. See [Telemetry](/docs/dashboard/telemetry).
