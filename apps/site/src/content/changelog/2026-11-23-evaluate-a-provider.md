---
title: Try a runner provider on your own workflows
date: 2026-11-23
type: Feature
description: Start an evaluation from any runner's page, run your real workflows on it, and compare the times as they finish.
---

- **Evaluate from the site.** **Evaluate** on [Integrations](/integrations), or **Try** on a runner's page, starts an evaluation with that runner picked. The first step connects GitHub, and RelayRunner reads your last 14 days of runs in the background, so you can start right away.
- **Results as runs finish.** Run and compare is one step: time left, the comparison and the runs chart fill in as each run finishes. Stop early and the runs that finished are still compared.
- **On the provider's page.** A provider's page in the dashboard lists its recent evaluations.
