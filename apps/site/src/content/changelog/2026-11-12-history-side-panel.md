---
title: Open runs and jobs in a side panel
date: 2026-11-12
---

Click a run on the dashboard's **History** page to see its jobs, runners, waits and run times in a side panel. Click a job to see its steps and where each one fell. The panel lives in the URL, so you can share a link to it.

Jobs on your own machines also show how much CPU and memory they used. See [Activity and history](/docs/dashboard/activity).
