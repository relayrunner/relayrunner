---
title: Clearer run times and job sizes for members
date: 2026-11-19
type: Feature
description: Read one workflow's run times on History, see whether Runners is current, and let members size jobs in repos they can push to.
---

- **Run times on History.** The chart shows one workflow at a time, the busiest first, as a median line with a band for the middle 80% of runs. Failures show as marks, and cancelled runs sit behind a legend toggle.
- **Live on Runners.** The **Runners** page says **Live** while it's current, with exact times on hover. Once it falls behind, it shows when it last updated.
- **Members can size jobs in their repos.** Members can change an auto job's size and first provider in repos they can push to on GitHub, since they could already change its runner in the workflow file. Workspace-wide settings stay with owners and admins. See [Roles](/docs/dashboard/workspaces#roles).
