---
title: Agents ask you before they stop something
date: 2026-11-24
type: Agents
description: In agents that can show a form, RelayRunner asks you to confirm a new runner order, a paused runner or AI turned off.
---

- **Confirm in your agent.** In clients that support it, the MCP server asks you directly before a change that stops or drops something: a new runner order, going back to the workspace order, pausing a runner, or turning AI off. The change happens only once you accept. Other clients work as before. See [Allow changes](/docs/reference/mcp#allow-changes).
