---
title: Send a crash report from the Mac app
date: 2026-11-24
type: Mac app
description: After a crash, RelayRunner for Mac 0.7.0 shows you the whole report and sends it only if you say so.
---

- **Ask, then send.** The next launch after the app or its CLI crashes offers to send a report. It shows all of it first: versions, macOS, the error and where in the code it happened, with no repos, paths or logs. See [Privacy](/docs/reference/privacy).
- **Anonymous by default.** Tick the box to include your name and email if you'd like a reply. RelayRunner for Mac 0.7.0, CLI 0.20.0.
