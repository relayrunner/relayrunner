---
title: Agents can change your routing
date: 2026-11-19
description: Let an AI agent change the runner order, job sizes, retries and AI, and pause runners, with every change in Activity.
---

- **Allow changes for an agent.** Turn on **Allow changes** for a connected agent under **Settings → Agents**, and it can change the runner order, the default and per-job sizes for auto jobs, automatic retries and AI, and pause or resume runners. Owners and admins turn it on, per workspace. The agent acts as you, so it can't change what your role can't. See [MCP server](/docs/reference/mcp#allow-changes).
- **Settings changes in Activity.** Changes to the runner order, sizes, retries and AI now show in **Activity**, with who made them and whether it was through an agent. Filter by **Settings** to see only those.
