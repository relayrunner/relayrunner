---
title: Export tables
date: 2026-11-26
type: Feature
description: Dashboard tables have an Export menu that saves the rows as CSV or JSON, or copies them as Markdown.
---

- **Save a table.** Tables on Usage, Pull requests, Providers, Performance and Jobs have an **Export** menu. **Download CSV** opens in a spreadsheet, and **Download JSON** suits a script.
- **Paste it anywhere.** **Copy as Markdown** puts the table on your clipboard, ready for a chat with a teammate or an agent.
- **What you see is what you get.** Exports follow the page's range, repo filter and sort, with full numbers and links to each pull request and job.
