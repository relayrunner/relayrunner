---
title: RelayRunner for Mac
date: 2026-11-17
description: A menu bar app that puts your Mac online with one switch, with the CLI built in.
---

- **One switch in the menu bar.** Turn your Mac on as a runner, pause new jobs and see what's running, without a terminal open. Each job still runs in its own Linux VM.
- **Settings for every `connect` option.** Jobs at once, VM CPUs and memory, macOS jobs, local network access and which workspaces the Mac takes jobs from.
- **Install.** [Download it](/mac/download) or run `brew install --cask relayrunner/tap/relayrunner`. It updates itself. Needs Apple silicon and macOS 14. See [RelayRunner for Mac](/docs/get-started/mac-app).
